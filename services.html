Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Essential Questions

Got questions about our design timelines, onboarding process, or automation compatibility?

How does the "Free Website Preview" work?
Simply fill out our contact form. Our design team will analyze your local roofing, plumbing, or landscaping market and build a custom high-fidelity homepage visual design draft tailored to your brand. We will showcase it to you on a demo link, free of charge. No obligations.
What platforms do you build on?
We write clean, semantic code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) directly, avoiding bulky visual editors like WordPress or Elementor. This guarantees 100/100 speed metrics. However, if your business relies on CRM portals like Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ServiceTitan, we build secure API endpoints to link estimate data directly.
Can my existing CRM connect to the AI receptionist?
Yes. Our AI receptionists and WhatsApp booking integrations are built using standard webhooks and open APIs. We support direct synching to major service CRMs including Jobber, Housecall Pro, Zapier, and Google Workspace sheets.
How long does a typical redesign take?
A standard contractor website development project takes between 2 to 3 weeks from final visual sign-off to server launch. If we are integrating custom WhatsApp bots or calendar schedulers, we typically launch the website first and deploy the automated booking pipelines within 5 additional working days.
